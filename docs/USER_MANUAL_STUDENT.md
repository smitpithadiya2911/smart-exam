# Student User Manual

## Overview
Students take proctored online examinations, track performance progress, download official PDF scorecards and QR certificates, view AI study recommendations, and earn 3D achievement badges.

### Key Workflows:
1. **Taking an Online Examination**:
   - Navigate to **Upcoming Exams** -> Select an active live exam -> Click **Start Examination**.
   - Review instructions, enter passcode if protected, and agree to anti-cheating rules.
   - In the Live Exam Room:
     - Use the color-coded **Question Palette** to navigate between questions.
     - Answers are **autosaved** in real time via AJAX.
     - Maintain full-screen mode and avoid switching tabs to prevent anti-cheat violation warnings.

2. **Results & QR Certificates**:
   - View detailed scorecards, question breakdown, rank, and download official PDF scorecards.
   - If qualified (passed), view and download your QR-verified certificate with 3D hover-tilt preview card.

3. **AI Learning Plan & Leaderboard**:
   - View personalized weak topic recommendations and predicted next scores under **AI Study Plan**.
   - Check top student rankings and unlocked 3D achievement medals under **Leaderboard**.
